ATTENDANCE POLICY
-----------------
1. All students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each course
   throughout the semester. Attendance is tracked per lecture, lab, and tutorial.

2. Students with attendance between 65%-74% will receive a formal warning
   letter from the Dean of Student Affairs.

3. Students with attendance below 65% will be debarred from the end-semester
   examination for that course.

4. Medical attendance condonation of up to 10% may be granted on submission
   of valid medical certificates to the Dean's office within 7 working days
   of resuming classes.

GRADING POLICY
--------------
The University follows an absolute grading system:

  Grade  | Marks Range | Grade Points
  -------|-------------|-------------
  O      | 90-100      | 10
  A+     | 80-89       | 9
  A      | 70-79       | 8
  B+     | 60-69       | 7
  B      | 50-59       | 6
  C      | 40-49       | 5
  F      | Below 40    | 0

Passing Criteria:
- Theory courses: Minimum grade of "C" (40 marks out of 100) to pass.
- Lab courses: Minimum grade of "C" and completion of all mandatory experiments.
- Projects/thesis: Minimum grade of "B" with submitted report and viva voce.

CGPA Calculation:
  CGPA = Sum of (Grade Points x Credits) / Total Credits
  A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required for degree conferment.

COURSE REGISTRATION
-------------------
- Registration opens 2 weeks before the semester starts.
- Last date for regular registration: End of the first week of the semester.
- Late registration with Rs. 500 late fee is allowed up to 2 weeks after
  the semester begins. No registration accepted after that.

Registration Deadlines for 2024-2025:
  Odd Semester:  Opens July 15, 2024. Last date: August 2, 2024.
  Even Semester: Opens January 5, 2025. Last date: January 20, 2025.

Maximum Credit Load:
  - Regular students: 24 credits per semester.
  - Students with CGPA above 8.0: 27 credits.
  - Students on academic probation (CGPA below 5.0): 18 credits.

EXAMINATION REGISTRATION
-------------------------
All students must register for end-semester exams separately from course
registration. Exam registration opens 4 weeks before the examination period.

Documents Required for Examination Registration:
  1. Valid University ID Card (original, not expired)
  2. Fee payment receipt for the current semester
  3. Attendance compliance certificate from the department (min 75%)
  4. No Dues Certificate from the Library (no overdue books)
  5. Lab completion certificate (for lab courses, signed by lab instructor)
  6. Course registration confirmation printout from the student portal
  7. Internal assessment marks acknowledgment (verify on portal)

Exam Registration Fees:
  Regular registration (within deadline): Rs. 1,000 per semester
  Late registration (up to 1 week after deadline): Rs. 1,500
  Supplementary/Re-examination: Rs. 500 per course

HALL TICKET
-----------
Hall tickets are generated electronically and available for download on the
student portal, typically 1 week before exams.

How to Download:
  1. Log in to the Student Portal.
  2. Navigate to "Examinations" > "Hall Ticket".
  3. Verify all details, download, and print on A4 paper.
  4. Get it attested by the Head of Department (stamp + signature).

Students MUST carry the printed, attested hall ticket and university ID
to every exam. Entry without a valid hall ticket is not permitted.

Duplicate Hall Ticket:
  1. Report the loss to the Examination Section.
  2. Submit application to the Controller of Examinations.
  3. Pay Rs. 200 as duplicate hall ticket fee.
  4. Issued within 2 working days, marked as "DUPLICATE".
