================================================================================
                    CAMPUS RULES & STUDENT HELPDESK FAQS
================================================================================

1. ATTENDANCE POLICY:
- Minimum required attendance is 75% in every theory and lab course.
- If attendance falls between 65% and 74%, a formal warning letter is issued.
- If attendance is below 65%, the student is debarred from taking end-semester exams.
- Medical condonation up to 10% is allowed with a doctor's certificate submitted within 7 working days.

2. GRADING SYSTEM:
- Grade O: 90-100 marks (10 grade points)
- Grade A+: 80-89 marks (9 grade points)
- Grade A: 70-79 marks (8 grade points)
- Grade B+: 60-69 marks (7 grade points)
- Grade B: 50-59 marks (6 grade points)
- Grade C: 40-49 marks (5 grade points, minimum passing grade)
- Grade F: Below 40 marks (Failed)
- Minimum CGPA required for graduation is 5.0.

3. BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE PROCEDURE:
- Step 1: Log in to the Student Portal (portal.university.edu).
- Step 2: Go to Student Services -> Certificate Requests -> Bonafide Certificate.
- Step 3: Select purpose (passport, scholarship, bank account, visa) and upload your student ID scan.
- Step 4: Pay fee of Rs. 100 per copy.
- Step 5: Processing time is 3 to 5 working days. Collect the physical copy from Room 102, Admin Block.

4. TRANSFER CERTIFICATE (TC) PROCEDURE:
- Clear all dues and get a No Dues Certificate from Library, Hostel, Accounts, Department, and Sports.
- Submit the No Dues Certificate along with original admission letter and ID card to the Registrar.
- Pay the fee of Rs. 500.
- Processing time is 7 to 10 working days. Collect from the Registrar's Office.

5. LIBRARY TIMINGS & BORROWING RULES:
- Regular hours: Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Sunday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM (Reading Room only).
- Extended exam hours: Monday to Saturday from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Sunday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- Undergraduates can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days.
- Late fine is Rs. 5 per day per book.

6. EXAMINATION REGISTRATION CHECKLIST:
- Students must register for exams 4 weeks before the exam date.
- Required documents to submit/verify:
  1. Valid University ID Card (original)
  2. Semester fee payment receipt
  3. Minimum 75% attendance compliance certificate
  4. No Dues Certificate from the Library
  5. Lab completion certificate signed by instructor
  6. Course registration confirmation printout
- Regular exam fee is Rs. 1,000 per semester. Late fee is Rs. 1,500.
