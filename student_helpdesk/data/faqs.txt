BONAFIDE CERTIFICATE
--------------------
Q: What is a Bonafide Certificate?
A: An official document certifying a student is currently enrolled at the
   university. Required for bank accounts, passport, visa, scholarships, loans.

Q: What is the procedure for applying for a Bonafide Certificate?
A: Follow these steps:
   1. Log in to the University Student Portal (https://portal.university.edu).
   2. Navigate to "Student Services" > "Certificate Requests".
   3. Select "Bonafide Certificate" from the dropdown.
   4. Fill in required details (purpose, number of copies).
   5. Upload a scanned copy of your valid university ID card.
   6. Submit and note the application reference number.
   7. Certificate is processed within 3-5 working days.
   8. Collect from Admin Office (Room 102, Admin Block) with original ID.

   Alternatively, submit a physical application at the Admin Office counter
   during working hours (9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday).
   Fee: Rs. 100 per copy (online or at accounts counter).

TRANSFER CERTIFICATE (TC)
--------------------------
Q: How to apply for a Transfer Certificate?
A: The TC is issued when a student permanently leaves the university.
   Procedure:
   1. Obtain a "No Dues Certificate" from:
      - Library (return all books)
      - Hostel Office (clear dues, vacate room)
      - Accounts Section (clear pending fees)
      - Department Office (return lab equipment)
      - Sports Department (return equipment if any)
   2. Submit the No Dues Certificate with a formal application to the Registrar.
   3. Attach: original admission letter, university ID card, all fee receipts,
      and 2 passport-size photographs.
   4. TC is processed within 7-10 working days.
   5. Collect from the Registrar's Office with valid government photo ID.
   Fee: Rs. 500.

FEE RECEIPT
-----------
Q: How can a fee receipt be obtained?
A: Method 1 (Online — Recommended):
   1. Log in to the Student Portal.
   2. Go to "Accounts" > "Fee Payment History".
   3. Select the semester/year.
   4. Click "Download Receipt" for a digitally signed PDF.

   Method 2 (Physical Copy):
   1. Visit Accounts Section (Room 201, Admin Block).
   2. Submit written request with enrollment number.
   3. Duplicate receipt issued within 2 working days.
   Fee for duplicate physical receipt: Rs. 50.
   Working Hours: 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
