================================================================================
GLOBAL ENTERPRISE HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY MANUAL
================================================================================
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability : All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees
Department    : Human Resources & Employee Relations

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE (VACATION POLICY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Entitlement and Accrual:
All full-time permanent employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues on a pro-rata monthly basis (approximately 1.67 days per full month of completed service).

1.2 Notice and Approval Procedure:
To ensure operational continuity, employees must submit annual leave requests at least two (2) weeks in advance via the HR Self-Service Portal. Requests must be approved by the employee's direct reporting manager before travel or time-off arrangements are finalized.

1.3 Carryover & Encashment:
A maximum of five (5) unused annual leave days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, provided they are utilized within the first quarter (Q1, ending March 31). Unused leave beyond this allowance will lapse automatically without financial encashment, unless mandated by local labor legislation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: PARENTAL LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Primary Caregiver Leave Entitlement:
The company provides twelve (12) weeks of fully paid parental leave to eligible primary caregivers following the birth of a child, adoption, or formal foster care placement.

2.2 Application and Required Documentation:
To qualify for paid parental leave, an employee must submit a formal application to the Human Resources Department at least four (4) weeks prior to the anticipated commencement date. The application must be accompanied by certified official verification:
  - For biological birth: A certified copy of the government birth certificate or hospital delivery discharge documentation.
  - For adoption / legal guardianship: Official legal adoption decree or certified placement order from a recognized agency.

2.3 Benefit Continuity:
During the 12-week parental leave period, all health insurance, retirement contribution matching, and seniority accruals will continue without interruption.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: SICK LEAVE & MEDICAL DOCUMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Annual Sick Leave Allowance:
Full-time employees receive ten (10) days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave is allocated at the commencement of each calendar year for genuine illness, medical treatments, or personal incapacitation.

3.2 Mandatory Medical Documentation:
Employees must notify their immediate supervisor by 09:00 AM on the first day of illness. Medical documentation (a formal, signed medical certificate from a licensed physician or healthcare practitioner) is strictly required if sick leave exceeds three (3) consecutive working days.

3.3 Failure to Furnish Proof:
Failure to provide certified medical documentation for absences exceeding 3 consecutive working days will result in the uncertified days being categorized as Unpaid Leave and may trigger disciplinary review under unauthorized absence guidelines.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: BENEFITS ELIGIBILITY & ENROLLMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Eligibility Waiting Period:
All regular, full-time employees become eligible for comprehensive company benefits after completing thirty (30) days of continuous employment with the company.

4.2 Covered Benefits:
Upon satisfying the 30-day continuous employment requirement, employees may enroll in:
  - Group Health Insurance (Comprehensive Medical, Dental, and Vision coverage).
  - Retirement Plan & Company Matching (401k / Provident Fund company contribution matching up to 5% of gross base salary).
  - Group Life and Long-Term Disability Insurance.

4.3 Enrollment Window:
Eligible employees have a strict thirty (30) day open enrollment window following their 30th day of employment to select plan options and designate beneficiaries.
